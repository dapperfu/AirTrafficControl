        AC-OCD004
        A blocked, garbled, or lost link does not create a clearance
        A lost link, a blocked transmission, or a garbled transmission shall not by itself give the aircraft a new clearance. The aircraft shall keep flying the last clearance it had already accepted, and it shall ask for a repeat or repeat its own call only when the link can still carry speech. Acceptance is a concept in which a fault leaves the last accepted maneuver in force and adds no new runway clearance.
        The mock must not treat silence or noise as a clearance.
