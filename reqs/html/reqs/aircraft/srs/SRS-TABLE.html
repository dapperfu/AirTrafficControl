        AC-SRS001
        Reject invalid TOML and do not fly
        Before an aircraft flies, the CSCI shall read its TOML file and shall refuse to construct the aircraft when the file is missing, unreadable, or fails the AC-IRS003 schema. Acceptance is the example file producing an aircraft for N123AB, and an empty file, a missing registration, a pattern value outside left or right, and a frequency of 100.0 each raising a configuration error with no aircraft object.
        A bad file is not a flyable aircraft.

        AC-SRS002
        Speak the full N-number on every transmission
        The CSCI shall speak the configured registration on every transmission it sends. N123AB shall be November one two three alpha bravo. Digit 9 shall be niner and digit 0 shall be zero. A registration with a leading zero, the letters I or O, or lowercase letters shall be rejected. Acceptance is that spoken form and the rejection of N0123, N123I, and n123ab.
        The full form is the only call sign this increment sends.

        AC-SRS003
        Render requests as facility, call sign, position, request
        A request shall be rendered as the facility, the full call sign, the position when the call is an initial contact or a position report, and then the request, separated by commas and ended with a period. Acceptance is the ATIS request for Example Tower and N123AB, and the pattern-entry request from the ramp with information alpha, both parsing back to the same kind and registration.
        One renderer is the source of the request order.
