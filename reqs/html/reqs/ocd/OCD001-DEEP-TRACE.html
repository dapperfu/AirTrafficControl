    • OCD001
    1. Local tower service replaces the manned tower for one configured airport OCD001 The AirTrafficControl system shall perform the local tower function for one airport described by that airport configuration. The service shall accept pilot requests for runway assignment, sequence position, landing, takeoff, hold short, line up and wait, and go-around, and it shall answer each request with a clearance or a refusal. Acceptance is a configured airport that issues a runway and a sequence to an aircraft inside its protected volume, and that refuses a request the separation guard rejects.
    • SSS001
      1. Aircraft shall not occupy the same runway protected volume SSS001 While an aircraft occupies a runway protected volume, the system shall refuse every other clearance that would place a second aircraft in that volume or in a volume configured as intersecting or dependent. Independent parallel runways are separate volumes. Acceptance is two aircraft and one runway where the second line-up, landing, or takeoff is refused until the first aircraft is airborne or clear, and the same refusal across a configured conflict pair.
      • SRS008
        1. Assign a runway only when designation, independence, and occupancy allow it SRS008 The Tower CSCI shall assign a runway to an aircraft only when the designation allows the requested operation, the runway is not closed, and the assignment does not conflict with an aircraft already occupying that runway or a conflicting runway. The assignment shall record the aircraft, the runway, and the operation. Acceptance is a successful assignment onto an empty both-use runway and a refusal onto that runway while another aircraft occupies a conflicting runway.
        • SDD002
          1. Clearance state machine and the TLA+ actions that match it SDD002 The clearance state machine shall use the phases out, queued, hold, occupying, and air. The actions shall be assign, sequence, hold short, line up and wait, takeoff, land, go-around, and handoff, and each action shall match a TLA+ action of the same name in AtcTower. Occupying means the aircraft is inside the protected volume. Acceptance is a review that every Erlang action name appears in the TLA+ spec and that a sampled transition produces the same phase change in both descriptions.
        • STD008
          1. Queue, landing, takeoff, and runway assignment path STD008 The test shall assign a both-use runway, sequence two aircraft, hold the second short, line the first up, clear the first for takeoff, and then clear the second to land only after the first is airborne. A landing attempted while the first still occupies the runway shall be refused. Acceptance is that ordered path and the one refusal.
      • SRS012
        1. Issue hold short, line up and wait, and go-around when needed to keep the runway exclusive SRS012 The Tower CSCI shall issue hold short when an aircraft may approach a runway but may not enter it, line up and wait when the aircraft may enter and must not roll, and go-around when an aircraft on approach must leave the protected volume without landing. Line up and wait shall be refused when the volume or a conflicting volume is already occupied. Go-around shall remove the aircraft from the protected volume. Acceptance is a held aircraft that is not occupying, a line-up that is refused while the runway is occupied, and a go-around that frees the runway for the next aircraft.

      • SRS013
        1. Apply the separation guard before any clearance that changes occupancy SRS013 Before committing a sequence, assignment, hold short, line up and wait, takeoff, landing, or go-around, the Tower CSCI shall evaluate the pure separation transition for that command. Only a transition that returns an accepted next state may be appended to the log. The guard shall be a pure function of the current occupancy, the runway relation, and the link state. Acceptance is a command set on which every accepted command preserves the exclusive-volume invariant and every rejected command leaves the state unchanged.
        • SDD003
          1. Refinement mapping from Erlang state to TLA+ state, including open obligations SDD003 The design shall include a refinement mapping from the Erlang clearance state to the TLA+ variables for phase, runway, sequence, and link status. The mapping shall list the Raft ordered-log assumption and every obligation that TLC and PropEr do not discharge, and those obligations shall remain labeled open. Acceptance is a mapping document linked from this requirement that a reader can use to find each Erlang field in the spec, and that does not describe an open obligation as proved.
        • STD009
          1. The guard refuses an unsafe clearance STD009 The test shall submit a line-up for a second aircraft while the first occupies the runway and expect a refusal with the state unchanged. It shall then go around or complete the takeoff and expect the second line-up to be accepted. Acceptance is the refusal with an unchanged log prefix and the later acceptance.
        • STD010
          1. PropEr state machine for concurrency, crash, recovery, and agreement STD010 The PropEr state machine shall generate sequences of clearance commands, concurrent submissions, a member crash, and a restart that replays the log. Every accepted sequence shall preserve the exclusive-volume invariant, and every crashed member that restarts shall match the surviving log prefix before it accepts a new command. A failing property shall fail the build. Acceptance is a PropEr run of this module that passes, and a deliberate invariant break that fails the property.
    • SSS002
      1. Each airport shall run on n ATC computers for integer n greater than 0 SSS002 Each airport shall be configured with an integer computer count n greater than 0 and shall start a local clearance group of that size. A configuration of one computer is a valid rural airport and shall still apply the separation guard. Acceptance is a startup with n equal to 1 that serves local traffic, and a startup that rejects n less than 1.
      • SRS003
        1. Form the local group of size n, including n equals 1 SRS003 On startup the Tower CSCI shall form a local computer group whose size is the configured n, including a group of one. Each member shall know the group size and its own identity. A node shall not issue clearances until it has joined that group. Acceptance is a one-member group that becomes the authority, and a three-member group that does not issue a clearance from a member that has not joined.
        • SDD001
          1. OTP supervision tree for one airport group SDD001 The Tower CSCI shall start under an OTP application and a one-for-one supervisor. The supervisor shall own the configuration loader, the local log, the neighbor monitor, and the clearance service. A child crash shall restart that child without creating a second log. Acceptance is an application start that registers one supervisor, and a killed child that is restarted while the committed log prefix is unchanged.
        • STD003
          1. Group membership for one computer and for three STD003 The test shall start a group with n equal to 1 and expect that member to be the authority, then start a group with n equal to 3 and expect a member that has not joined to refuse a clearance. Acceptance is authority on the single member and refusal from the unjoined member.
      • SSDD001
        1. Airport, local computers, and neighbor topology, with the two failure domains SSDD001 The system design shall separate two failure domains. Inside an airport, n computers share one ordered log and lose the authority only when they lose a majority. Between airports, a link failure isolates the airports and each continues with local service under the fail-smart rule. A local computer failure shall not be treated as a neighbor-link failure, and a neighbor-link failure shall not by itself remove a local majority. Acceptance is a design trace that names both domains and a test in which a down neighbor leaves the local majority able to clear a local aircraft.
    • SSS006
      1. Runways and their designations shall come from one TOML file per airport SSS006 Each airport shall be configured by one TOML file that names the airport, its position, communications distance, computer count, envelope bounds, and every runway. Each runway shall have an identifier, a designation of arrival, departure, both, or closed, and a flag stating whether it is independent of the other runways. The system shall not control traffic from a missing or invalid file. Acceptance is a file edit that closes a runway and a following assignment to that runway being refused.
      • IRS003
        1. TOML airport and runway schema IRS003 The configuration interface shall be one TOML document with an airport table containing identity, latitude, longitude, communications distance, computer count, and envelope limits, plus one runway table per runway containing identifier, designation, and independence. A runway may list the identifiers it intersects. Acceptance is the example airport file, which this schema accepts, and the same file with the designation removed, which it rejects.
        • IDD001
          1. Concrete schemas for IRS001 through IRS004 IDD001 The design shall represent a pilot clearance as a map with aircraft, runway, sequence, kind, wind, and text fields. A neighbor message shall be a tuple of request, accept, reject, or stale with the aircraft identity. The TOML schema shall match IRS003. A log command shall be a tuple of the clearance kind and its arguments. Acceptance is a round trip that encodes one landing clearance and one handoff accept and decodes them to the same fields.
      • SRS001
        1. Reject invalid TOML and do not control traffic SRS001 Before the Tower CSCI controls traffic it shall read the airport TOML file and shall refuse to start control when the file is missing, unreadable, or lacks a required airport identity, position, communications distance, computer count, or runway list. A runway entry that lacks an identifier or a designation shall also prevent control. Acceptance is an empty file and a file with a runway and no designation, both of which leave the node with no authority to issue a clearance.
        • STD001
          1. TOML accept and reject cases STD001 The test shall load the example airport TOML and expect a parsed airport with its runways, then load an empty file and a runway without a designation and expect both to be rejected before any clearance is issued. Acceptance is those three results, with the valid file producing a runway list and the two invalid files producing an error and no authority.
      • SRS002
        1. Use runway designation and independence when assigning a runway SRS002 The Tower CSCI shall assign a runway only when its designation allows the intended operation: arrival for landing, departure for takeoff, both for either, and closed for neither. A runway marked not independent shall conflict with every other runway that is not independent, and a runway listed as intersecting another shall conflict with that runway. Acceptance is a closed runway that cannot be assigned, a departure-only runway that rejects a landing, and two non-independent runways that cannot be occupied together.
        • STD002
          1. Runway designation selection cases STD002 The test shall attempt a landing on a departure-only runway, a takeoff on an arrival-only runway, an assignment to a closed runway, and a dual occupancy of two non-independent runways. Each attempt shall be refused. A landing on an empty both-use runway shall be accepted. Acceptance is those five outcomes.
    • SSS007
      1. Separation shall be demonstrated by a TLA+ model under stated assumptions and bounds SSS007 The separation invariant and the admissible clearance transitions shall be specified in TLA+ and checked with TLC on a finite model of at most 4 runways, 8 aircraft, and 2 neighbor airports. Every admissible transition in that model shall preserve exclusive protected volume, dependent-runway exclusion, and queue order, and shall refuse a neighbor-dependent clearance while the neighbor link is down. The software shall refuse to control a traffic picture outside that envelope. Acceptance is a TLC run that reports success on the checked model and a startup rejection above the envelope.
      • SRS014
        1. Refuse operation outside the performance assumptions and the checked envelope SRS014 The Tower CSCI shall read the configured maximum runways, aircraft, and neighbors and shall refuse to start control when any of those limits exceeds 4 runways, 8 aircraft, or 2 neighbors, or when the loaded picture already exceeds the limit. It shall also refuse a new aircraft that would exceed the aircraft limit. Acceptance is a TOML file with 5 runways that does not start, and an airport at the aircraft limit that refuses one more aircraft.
        • STD011
          1. Bounds refusal STD011 The test shall reject a configuration with 5 runways, reject a configuration that allows more than 8 aircraft, and reject the ninth aircraft at an airport already holding 8. Acceptance is those three refusals and no issued clearance in the first two cases.
    • SSS008
      1. The Erlang service shall be tied to that model by a refinement argument SSS008 The Erlang clearance state shall be related to the TLA+ state by a written refinement mapping that names the corresponding variables and the assumptions it depends on, including the single ordered log. PropEr state-machine tests shall sample the Erlang transitions, including concurrent requests, process failure, recovery, and replica agreement, and those tests shall be recorded as conformance evidence rather than as a proof. Any obligation the mapping does not discharge shall stay labeled open. Acceptance is a mapping that a reader can check against the module and the spec, plus a test run that fails the build when a sampled transition breaks the invariant.

    • SSS009
      1. When n is greater than 1, one local computer failure shall leave a single clearance authority SSS009 When more than one computer is configured, clearances shall be appended to one ordered log and only a majority member that is the elected authority may append. After one computer fails, a surviving majority shall keep exactly one authority and the failed computer shall not issue clearances. For two computers the majority is both, so one failure stops clearances instead of leaving two authorities. Acceptance is a group of three where killing one leaves one authority, and a partition into one and two where only the side of two issues a clearance.

Members shall apply a committed command at the same index to the same previous state. Acceptance is two members that apply one landing command and report the same resulting occupancy.

        • SDD004

        • STD004

Acceptance is one committed clearance after the crash and a refusal from the minority side.
