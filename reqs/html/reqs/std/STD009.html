    1. The guard refuses an unsafe clearance
    UID: STD009
    DAL: A
    REQUIREMENT_LEVEL: Low-Level
    VERIFICATION: Test
    RELATIONS (Parent):
    • STP001 Which SRS items are discharged by TLC, by refinement review, by PropEr, and by example tests
    • SRS013 Apply the separation guard before any clearance that changes occupancy
    RELATIONS (File):
    • apps/atc_tower/test/atc_clearance_SUITE.erl, lines: 1-112, entire file
    STATEMENT: The test shall submit a line-up for a second aircraft while the first occupies the runway and expect a refusal with the state unchanged. It shall then go around or complete the takeoff and expect the second line-up to be accepted. Acceptance is the refusal with an unchanged log prefix and the later acceptance.
    RATIONALE: The guard requirement is checked here by example, and by TLC and PropEr under the other descriptions.
    ASSUMPTIONS: The checked envelope is 4 runways, 8 aircraft under control, and 2 neighbor airports. Visual separation, LAHSO, radar vectoring, TRACON, enroute control, and wake RECAT are outside this increment.
