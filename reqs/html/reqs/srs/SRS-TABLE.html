        SRS001
        Reject invalid TOML and do not control traffic
        Before the Tower CSCI controls traffic it shall read the airport TOML file and shall refuse to start control when the file is missing, unreadable, or lacks a required airport identity, position, communications distance, computer count, or runway list. A runway entry that lacks an identifier or a designation shall also prevent control. Acceptance is an empty file and a file with a runway and no designation, both of which leave the node with no authority to issue a clearance.
        A bad configuration is not a safe airport, so the node must not become a clearance authority.
        A
        High-Level
        Test
        The file is TOML. Unknown keys may be ignored. Missing required keys are errors.

        SRS002
        Use runway designation and independence when assigning a runway
        The Tower CSCI shall assign a runway only when its designation allows the intended operation: arrival for landing, departure for takeoff, both for either, and closed for neither. A runway marked not independent shall conflict with every other runway that is not independent, and a runway listed as intersecting another shall conflict with that runway. Acceptance is a closed runway that cannot be assigned, a departure-only runway that rejects a landing, and two non-independent runways that cannot be occupied together.
        Designation is the policy in the TOML file. The clearance code must not invent a use the file does not allow.
        A

        SRS003
        Form the local group of size n, including n equals 1
        On startup the Tower CSCI shall form a local computer group whose size is the configured n, including a group of one. Each member shall know the group size and its own identity. A node shall not issue clearances until it has joined that group. Acceptance is a one-member group that becomes the authority, and a three-member group that does not issue a clearance from a member that has not joined.
        The group is the unit of local redundancy. One member is the rural case and is still a group.

        SRS004
        Replicate clearance state as one ordered log
        Issued clearances shall be the prefix of one ordered log replicated to the local group. A member shall apply the same command to the same log index as every other member that holds that index. A command that the guard rejects shall not extend the log. Acceptance is two members that, after the same committed prefix, report the same occupancy, and a rejected command that leaves both prefixes unchanged.
        One ordered history is the software view of the single clearance authority.

        SRS005
        Select neighbors by communications distance
        The Tower CSCI shall compute the distance between this airport and each configured candidate and shall mark a candidate as a neighbor only when that distance is less than or equal to the configured communications distance. A candidate with no position shall not become a neighbor. Acceptance is a candidate one unit inside the distance that is selected and a candidate one unit outside that is not.
        Neighbor selection is data, computed from the TOML positions and distance, not a hard-coded airport list in the clearance module.

        SRS006
        Complete a handoff only with a reachable neighbor
        The Tower CSCI shall send a handoff only to a neighbor whose link is up, and it shall remove the aircraft from local control only after that neighbor accepts. A reject or a missing answer shall leave the aircraft under local control. Acceptance is an accepted handoff that drops local control, and a rejected handoff that keeps the aircraft in the local sequence.
        The sender must not drop an aircraft the receiver has not taken.

        SRS007
        On a dead neighbor link, continue local service and refuse dependent clearances
        When a neighbor link is down, the Tower CSCI shall keep serving aircraft in the local volume and shall refuse a handoff or any clearance whose safety would depend on that neighbor. State last received from that neighbor shall be marked stale and shall not be used to permit a tighter local operation. Acceptance is a down link during which a local landing is still granted when the local guard allows it, a handoff is refused, and stale neighbor occupancy is not treated as empty sky.
        This is the software statement of fail-smart behavior for a partitioned airport.

        SRS008
        Assign a runway only when designation, independence, and occupancy allow it
        The Tower CSCI shall assign a runway to an aircraft only when the designation allows the requested operation, the runway is not closed, and the assignment does not conflict with an aircraft already occupying that runway or a conflicting runway. The assignment shall record the aircraft, the runway, and the operation. Acceptance is a successful assignment onto an empty both-use runway and a refusal onto that runway while another aircraft occupies a conflicting runway.
        Assignment is the first occupancy decision and it has to obey the same exclusion as a takeoff or landing.

        SRS009
        Keep arrival and departure sequence order
        The Tower CSCI shall keep a sequence per runway and shall grant a landing or takeoff that uses that runway only to the aircraft at the head of the sequence, after any predecessor has left the protected volume. A new sequence request shall append the aircraft. Acceptance is two aircraft sequenced to one runway where the second takeoff or landing is refused until the first is airborne or clear, then granted.
        Queue order is the in-trail rule this increment can check without a wake-turbulence model.
        A
        High-Level
        Test
        The in-trail gap is the predecessor leaving the protected volume. No time-based wake category is modeled.

        SRS012
        Issue hold short, line up and wait, and go-around when needed to keep the runway exclusive
        The Tower CSCI shall issue hold short when an aircraft may approach a runway but may not enter it, line up and wait when the aircraft may enter and must not roll, and go-around when an aircraft on approach must leave the protected volume without landing. Line up and wait shall be refused when the volume or a conflicting volume is already occupied. Go-around shall remove the aircraft from the protected volume. Acceptance is a held aircraft that is not occupying, a line-up that is refused while the runway is occupied, and a go-around that frees the runway for the next aircraft.
        These three clearances are the control the tower needs to keep the exclusive-volume invariant enforceable.

        SRS013
        Apply the separation guard before any clearance that changes occupancy
        Before committing a sequence, assignment, hold short, line up and wait, takeoff, landing, or go-around, the Tower CSCI shall evaluate the pure separation transition for that command. Only a transition that returns an accepted next state may be appended to the log. The guard shall be a pure function of the current occupancy, the runway relation, and the link state. Acceptance is a command set on which every accepted command preserves the exclusive-volume invariant and every rejected command leaves the state unchanged.
        The guard is the executable counterpart of the TLA+ next-state relation.
