      1. Loss of the outside link shall fail smart and preserve local separation UID: SSS004 DAL: A REQUIREMENT_LEVEL: System VERIFICATION: Test RELATIONS (Parent):
      • OCD003 A rural airport keeps working when the outside link is down
      RELATIONS (Child):
      • SRS007 On a dead neighbor link, continue local service and refuse dependent clearances
      STATEMENT: When an airport can no longer exchange traffic state with a neighbor inside the configured communications distance, the local computers shall keep runway assignment, sequencing, landing, and takeoff service for aircraft already inside that airport's protected volume. The airport shall not issue a handoff or any other clearance that depends on neighbor state it cannot confirm. Last-known neighbor data shall stay marked stale and shall not reduce local separation. Coordinated handoffs shall resume only after the neighbor link is revalidated. This shall hold for a rural airport with one computer and no reachable neighbor.
       RATIONALE: Fail smart means the local tower continues and unconfirmed neighbor state cannot make a local clearance look safer.
       ASSUMPTIONS: The checked envelope is 4 runways, 8 aircraft under control, and 2 neighbor airports. Visual separation, LAHSO, radar vectoring, TRACON, enroute control, and wake RECAT are outside this increment.

    STATEMENT: When a neighbor link is down, the Tower CSCI shall keep serving aircraft in the local volume and shall refuse a handoff or any clearance whose safety would depend on that neighbor. State last received from that neighbor shall be marked stale and shall not be used to permit a tighter local operation. Acceptance is a down link during which a local landing is still granted when the local guard allows it, a handoff is refused, and stale neighbor occupancy is not treated as empty sky.
     RATIONALE: This is the software statement of fail-smart behavior for a partitioned airport.
     ASSUMPTIONS: The checked envelope is 4 runways, 8 aircraft under control, and 2 neighbor airports. Visual separation, LAHSO, radar vectoring, TRACON, enroute control, and wake RECAT are outside this increment.
    • SDD005
      1. Fail-smart mode and the stale-neighbor rule UID: SDD005 DAL: A REQUIREMENT_LEVEL: Low-Level VERIFICATION: Test RELATIONS (Parent):
      • SRS007 On a dead neighbor link, continue local service and refuse dependent clearances
      RELATIONS (File):
      • apps/atc_tower/src/atc_neighbors.erl, lines: 1-50, entire file
      STATEMENT: Neighbor state shall be a record with a link flag and a stale flag. When the link is down the stale flag shall be set and the handoff action shall be disabled. Local clearance actions shall ignore stale neighbor occupancy when deciding whether a local volume is free. Acceptance is a neighbor record that becomes stale on link loss and a local landing that is still decided only from local occupancy.
       RATIONALE: Stale data remains visible for the operator and unusable for reducing separation.
       ASSUMPTIONS: The checked envelope is 4 runways, 8 aircraft under control, and 2 neighbor airports. Visual separation, LAHSO, radar vectoring, TRACON, enroute control, and wake RECAT are outside this increment.

      STATEMENT: The test shall bring a neighbor link down, mark the neighbor picture stale, grant a local landing that the local guard allows, and refuse a handoff. It shall also show that the stale picture is not used to permit a second aircraft into a locally occupied volume. Acceptance is the granted local landing, the refused handoff, and the refused double occupancy.
       RATIONALE: Fail smart is continued local service plus refusal of dependent clearances.
       ASSUMPTIONS: The checked envelope is 4 runways, 8 aircraft under control, and 2 neighbor airports. Visual separation, LAHSO, radar vectoring, TRACON, enroute control, and wake RECAT are outside this increment.
