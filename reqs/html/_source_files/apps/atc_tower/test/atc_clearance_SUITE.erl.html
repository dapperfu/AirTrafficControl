STATEMENT: The Tower CSCI shall assign a runway only when its designation allows the intended operation: arrival for landing, departure for takeoff, both for either, and closed for neither. A runway marked not independent shall conflict with every other runway that is not independent, and a runway listed as intersecting another shall conflict with that runway. Acceptance is a closed runway that cannot be assigned, a departure-only runway that rejects a landing, and two non-independent runways that cannot be occupied together.
