STATEMENT: The system shall publish a truth track for each simulated aircraft at every motion step. The track shall carry the aircraft identity, position, velocity, altitude, wake class, and time. Publishing a track shall not by itself issue a clearance. Acceptance is two aircraft whose tracks update on the same step and stay distinct, with the issued-clearance log unchanged by the publication.
 RATIONALE: Routine prediction needs one shared picture of where each aircraft is. The picture is not a clearance.
 ASSUMPTIONS: The checked envelope is 4 runways, 8 aircraft under control, and 2 neighbor airports. A training profile may publish tracks for a larger picture and does not change the checked envelope. This is simulation software, with no live NAS connection and no certification claim.
