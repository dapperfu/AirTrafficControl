STATEMENT: The checked profile shall remain 4 runways, 8 aircraft, and 2 neighbor airports for checked startup and for the TLA+ model. A training profile may run a larger picture and shall not change those checked limits. Acceptance is a checked configuration that refuses a ninth aircraft, and a training picture of more than 8 aircraft that leaves the checked limits at 4, 8, and 2.
 RATIONALE: The finite model check stays meaningful only if a busy training day does not pretend to be the proved envelope.
 ASSUMPTIONS: The checked envelope is 4 runways, 8 aircraft under control, and 2 neighbor airports. Raft is assumed to deliver one ordered log and is not re-proved. Visual separation, LAHSO, radar vectoring, TRACON, enroute control, and wake RECAT are outside the checked model.
