STATEMENT: The Tower CSCI shall auto-issue a routine taxi, hold-short, line-up, takeoff, landing, or vacate only by proposing a command that the separation guard accepts. The proposal shall be made only after the conflict probe reports safe. A scripted radio reply shall not be a second writer of that clearance. Acceptance is a taxi request that becomes an assign or hold-short only through the guard, and a conflicting request that is absent from the issued log.
 RATIONALE: Routine issuance is a suggestion the occupancy guard still has to commit.
 ASSUMPTIONS: The checked envelope is 4 runways, 8 aircraft under control, and 2 neighbor airports. Commands the guard does not know are not issued.

STATEMENT: The system shall propose a routine taxi, hold-short, line-up, takeoff, landing, vacate, or handoff only when the conflict probe reports the case safe and the separation guard would accept that same command. The separation guard shall remain the only writer of an issued clearance. A scripted radio sentence shall not record an issued clearance. Acceptance is a routine takeoff that appears on the guard log only after both checks agree, and a conflicting request that does not appear as issued.
 RATIONALE: The occupancy guard is the clearance authority. Prediction and phraseology may only offer a command that authority can accept.
 ASSUMPTIONS: The checked envelope is 4 runways, 8 aircraft under control, and 2 neighbor airports. Phraseology is a project-owned grammar. FAA order tables are not copied into the software.
