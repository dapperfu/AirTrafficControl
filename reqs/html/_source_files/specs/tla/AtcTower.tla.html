STATEMENT: While an aircraft occupies a runway protected volume, the system shall refuse every other clearance that would place a second aircraft in that volume or in a volume configured as intersecting or dependent. Independent parallel runways are separate volumes. Acceptance is two aircraft and one runway where the second line-up, landing, or takeoff is refused until the first aircraft is airborne or clear, and the same refusal across a configured conflict pair.
 RATIONALE: Loss of runway separation is the catastrophic condition this increment exists to prevent.
 ASSUMPTIONS: The checked envelope is 4 runways, 8 aircraft under control, and 2 neighbor airports. Visual separation, LAHSO, radar vectoring, TRACON, enroute control, and wake RECAT are outside this increment.

STATEMENT: Before committing a sequence, assignment, hold short, line up and wait, takeoff, landing, or go-around, the Tower CSCI shall evaluate the pure separation transition for that command. Only a transition that returns an accepted next state may be appended to the log. The guard shall be a pure function of the current occupancy, the runway relation, and the link state.
